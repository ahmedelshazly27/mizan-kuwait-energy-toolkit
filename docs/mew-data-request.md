# Data request to the Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy

*Draft for Mizan Energy to send. Suggested recipients: MEW's National Control
Center, through the Ministry's statistics department (the publisher of the
Statistical Year Book and the Monthly Statistics Book).*

---

**Subject: Request for hourly system load data for research use**

Dear Sir or Madam,

Mizan Energy maintains the Kuwait Electricity Open Dataset, a public, cited
record of the Ministry's published electricity statistics. It contains every value
we could extract from the Statistical Year Books (2020–2023 editions) and the
Monthly Statistics Books, each traced to its page, alongside the Central
Statistical Bureau's environment statistics. Our open-source Kuwait Energy Toolkit
uses these statistics to evaluate solar and storage plants under Kuwaiti
conditions.

The Ministry publishes system load on the yearly peak day and daily peak and
minimum loads for some months. For all other hours we currently rely on a model. We
would be grateful for:

1. **Hourly (or half-hourly) total system load**, in MW, for as many complete
   calendar years as can be shared. Any single recent year would already be
   valuable, and 2023 or 2024 would be most useful.
2. If available, the same series split into **generation and GCC interchange**.
3. A note on any **load-shedding periods** in the years provided, so that served
   load is not mistaken for demand.

We will cite the Ministry as the source in every use, and keep the data under any
terms the Ministry sets. If publication is not permitted, we will use it only to
validate our model and publish the validation results, not the data. We would
gladly share the toolkit's results with the Ministry.

Similar data has been provided to researchers before, for example for a published
study of COVID-19's effect on Kuwait's grid.

With thanks,
